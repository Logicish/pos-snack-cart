Owner & admin manual

Snack Cart

A self-contained point-of-sale terminal for a workplace snack cart. People scan their badge, scan their snacks, and pay the cart's owner by Venmo, Zelle, or Cash App from a QR code on the screen. Everything is stored on the SD card inside; nothing goes to the internet.

Firmware launch build, Oct 2026 Screen 3.5″ portrait Input 6 buttons + barcode scanner Power 5 V USB

01Overview

The cart runs on trust. There is no cash drawer and no card reader: each purchase is recorded against the buyer's badge as an unpaid transaction, and the buyer pays from their own phone. An admin marks transactions paid once the money arrives.

What's on the cart

  • Screen on the top panel, on the left.
  • Six buttons on the top panel, to the right of the screen: four yellow direction buttons in a diamond, with green (Enter) and red (Back) below them.
  • Barcode scanner on the front face. It reads both employee badges and product barcodes, and beeps on every good read.
  • Power switch, and a USB power cable from a wall adapter.
  • SD card slot, reachable through a cutout in the side of the case. All data lives on this card.

03Buying snacks

The everyday flow. The Start screen reads “Scan Badge To Start.”

Make a purchase

  1. Scan your badge. Your transaction opens with your name in the header.
  2. Scan each snack. Scanning the same item twice adds a second one. For an item with no barcode, press ▶ Item Lookup and pick it from the list.
  3. Fix mistakes by highlighting a line with ▲▼ and pressing ◀ Delete Item (removes one at a time).
  4. Press ENTER Pay. The Payment screen shows the total and a QR code. Press ▶ Other Payment to switch method.
  5. Pay from your phone (see below), then press ENTER Confirm. The sale is saved and the cart returns to Start.

BACK on the cart or Payment screen cancels the whole transaction (it asks first on the cart screen). Nothing is saved until Confirm.

New here?

When Auto Enroll is on, scanning an unknown badge asks “Badge not found. Enroll new user?” Press ENTER Yes, spell your first and last name on the letter wheel, then scan your badge once more to confirm. Your first transaction opens right away. If Auto Enroll is off, an admin adds you (Admin Menu → Users → Add User).

From the Start screen

PressOpens
▶Browse/Price: the full price list; scan any item to see its price. Nothing is charged.
◀Extras: scan your badge, then choose Check Balance (your own unpaid transactions, with the QR code again in case a payment didn't go through), Laggy Fish, or Slide Free. The games keep a best score per person.

Screensaver. After a few idle minutes the screen dims and a fish wanders around. Press any button to wake it. The scanner is switched off while it's showing, so wake the cart before scanning.

Auto logout. A transaction or admin screen left alone returns to Start on its own after 5 minutes (adjustable). An unfinished transaction is discarded, not saved.

Balances: marking payments received

One row per unpaid transaction, with its number, person, and amount. When the money arrives, highlight the row and press ENTER Clear, then ENTER Yes. Cleared transactions leave the list but stay in the lifetime reports. Venmo payments carry the transaction number in their note; match Zelle and Cash App payments by amount and time.

Inventory

  • Restock: scan an item, adjust its count, confirm.
  • Add/Attach Item: scan a barcode the cart doesn't know. Either attach it to an existing item (useful for a variety pack sold as one item) or create a new item with a name and price. An item can have up to 8 barcodes.
  • Inventory: the full list. Open an item to change its price or stock, link or unlink barcodes, hide it (off Browse, and its barcode scans as unknown, but its sales history is kept), or delete it. Items that have ever sold can only be hidden, not deleted.

Stock goes down automatically with every sale, and can go below zero if the count was off. For entering many items at once, the web portal is quicker than the buttons.

Users

  • Add User: scan the new person's badge, spell their name, and choose whether they're an admin.
  • Edit Users: rename someone, make them an admin, or set them inactive. An inactive person's badge is turned away at the cart, but their history is kept.
  • Auto Enroll: when on, unknown badges can sign themselves up (see Buying snacks).

You can't change your own account while you're logged in, on the cart or on the web. This guarantees there's always at least one working admin. Ask another admin to make changes to your account.

05Web portal

A phone-friendly admin site served by the cart itself, over its own WiFi network.

  1. Log in as an admin and open Web Portal. The cart's WiFi switches on and the screen shows the network name (SnackCart), its password, and a QR code for the page.
  2. Join that network on your phone, then scan the QR code (or browse to the address shown).
  3. Work in the browser. Keep the Web Portal screen open on the cart the whole time; the WiFi turns off the moment you leave that screen.
PageWhat's there
HomeOutstanding balance, unpaid transactions, people and item counts.
BalanceUnpaid transactions grouped by person, each with a Clear button.
ItemsEvery item with price and stock, quick ±1/±10 stock buttons, Edit (name, price, hidden, delete), and Add Item.
ReportLifetime and monthly sales, per person and per item. Downloads as a spreadsheet (.xlsx) or plain text, for unpaid only, the last 30 days, or lifetime.
UsersAdmin and Active switches per person, and Edit (name, badge number, registration date, delete).
AdminCart settings, clock, WiFi password, payment accounts (view only), Back up now, Download database, Download boot log.

The portal has no login of its own. Access is protected by needing an admin badge at the cart and the WiFi password, and by the WiFi only running while that screen is open. Anyone on the network while it's open can see and change everything, so close the Web Portal screen when you're done.

06Care & backups

How your data is protected

  • Every power-on checks the whole database before using it.
  • The cart keeps five rolling backup copies on the SD card, refreshed at every power-on, after every sale, and when the web portal closes. Each copy is read back and compared before it replaces an older one, and a damaged database is never copied over a good backup.
  • If the database is missing or damaged at power-on, the cart restores the newest good copy by itself and shows an alert.

All five copies are on the same SD card. If the card itself dies, they go with it. Every so often, use Download database on the web portal's Admin page to keep a copy on your phone or computer.

Removing the SD card

  1. Turn the cart off with the power switch. Never pull the card while it's on.
  2. Press the card in through the side cutout to release it.
  3. On a computer, the card shows the database (pos.db), its backups, the boot log, and the Slide Free puzzle files. Don't rename or delete pos.db-journal; the database uses it to recover from power cuts.

Recovering on a new card

  1. Format a card as FAT32. Testing a new card first with a free tool such as H2testw catches bad or fake-capacity cards.
  2. Copy your most recent downloaded database onto it and rename it pos.db. Copy the slidefree folder too if you have it.
  3. Insert it and power on. Everything returns as of that download.

A completely blank card starts a setup wizard instead, which walks you through scanning in the first admin. Items and people then have to be added again.

Cleaning & placement

  • Wipe the screen and scanner window with a soft, dry or barely damp cloth. Don't spray liquid onto the cart.
  • Keep the vent holes clear, and keep the cart out of direct sun.
  • The base is weighted so the cart doesn't tip forward; set it on a flat surface.

07Alerts & boot log

Every time it powers on, the cart checks itself and writes one line to boot_log.csv on the SD card. Problems also appear as a yellow bar at the top of the Admin Menu; customers never see it. The bar shows the most important problem, with “(+N more)” if there are others.

To read the log, open Advanced Tools → Boot Log (◀ older, ▶ newer, ▲▼ scroll; failures show in red, things to act on in orange), or use Download boot log on the web portal.

AlertWhat to do
NO SD CARD DETECTEDPower off, reseat the card through the side cutout, power on.
DATABASE UNAVAILABLEThe database couldn't be opened or restored. Try another card with a downloaded copy (see Care & backups).
DB RESTORED FROM BACKUP THIS BOOTThe cart repaired itself. Anything since the last backup (usually only the very last sale) may be missing; check Balances.
UNEXPECTED RESTART: BROWNOUTThe power dipped. Try a stronger USB adapter (2 A or more) or a different cable.
UNEXPECTED RESTART: CRASH / WATCHDOGThe software stopped. Note what was happening and check the boot log; if it repeats, it needs a firmware fix.
SD CARD NOT WRITABLECheck the card's lock switch; replace the card if it persists.
LOW SD CARD SPACEUnder 5% free. Use a larger card.
CLOCK IS WRONG - SET CLOCK
CLOCK IS BEHIND LAST SALE		Settings → Set Clock.
CLOCK BATTERY LOST POWERSet the clock. If it keeps happening, replace the clock's CR2032 coin cell.
CLOCK CHIP NOT FOUNDHardware fault in the clock wiring. The cart still runs, but times will be wrong after every power cut.
SCANNER NOT RESPONDINGPower off and on. If it persists, the scanner cable needs checking.

SCANNER SETTINGS DIDN'T STICK		Power off and on. If it repeats, Advanced Tools → Scanner → Restore Defaults.

A rising SD write time in the boot log over several months is an early sign of a wearing card; compare power-on boots with each other (a cold power-on is normally around 250 ms).

  • Recommended supply: a 5 V USB wall adapter rated 2 A or more. Peaks last milliseconds, but a weak adapter or thin cable can let the voltage dip and restart the cart (logged as a brownout).
  • Daily use: at roughly 1 W average, about 24 Wh a day, or about 0.7 kWh a month: on the order of ten cents of electricity.
  • Clock battery: the DS3231 draws only microamps from its CR2032 when the cart is unplugged; expect years of life.

Software

  • Arduino framework on PlatformIO, LVGL 8.4 graphics, SQLite database on the SD card.

  • Times are stored as local time with no time zone or daylight-saving adjustment.
